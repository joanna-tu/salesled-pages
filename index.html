Account X-ray

Your AI, doing your first-call prep.

Open Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini*, or whichever AI you use. Send it one message:

I work at [your company] and I'm meeting [person] at [account]. xray.joannatu.com/full

That's the whole setup. No form, no prompt to copy.

What comes back

A 10-line meeting card you can read in the lift. Then the dossier: the money, what just changed, who's in the room, the tool you'd be replacing. It closes with 5 to 7 angles to open with.

What it looks for

Signals across filings, hiring, leadership moves, tech changes, expansion, reviews. Each one gets three questions: is it my problem, is it fresh, who owns the decision. Then a verdict: act now, this week, or watch.

Why you can trust it

The method is the page. Every rule and every score is written out - read it, run it, steal it. It runs in the AI you already pay for, no signup. And it grades itself: sixteen checks against stale or invented findings, and it says so when it's guessing.
