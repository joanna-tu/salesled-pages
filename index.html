Account X-ray

First-call account research, run by whatever AI you already use. Made by Joanna Turczynska.

The process is simple.

Step 1: Give your AI this link >> xray.joannatu.com/standard << and tell it to run the page exactly as written.

Step 2: Tell it your company, and who you're meeting.

Step 3: Get the meeting card and the dossier. Sound like you've been watching this account for months.

One line does steps 1 and 2 together - paste this:

Open this link and run the page exactly as written: https://xray.joannatu.com/standard/. My company: X. Meeting: Y at Z.

Small print: the full edition adds the complete 39-signal catalog. Gemini usually cannot open links - copy the page text instead, same line on top.
