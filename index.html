What it does

One run builds your first-call prep: a 10-line meeting card, then the full dossier - the money, the momentum, the people in the room, the tool you'd be replacing - and closes with 5-7 angles for outreach.

What it reads

39 signals across filings, hiring, leadership moves, tech changes, expansion, and reviews. Each one gets three questions: is it my problem, is it fresh, who owns the decision. Then a tier: act now, this week, or watch.

Why it's different

The method is the page. Every rule and every score is written out - read it, run it, steal it. It runs on the AI you already use, no signup. And it audits itself: sixteen checks against stale or invented findings before you trust a word.
