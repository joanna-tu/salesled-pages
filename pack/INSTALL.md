# Account X-ray - the guide

Three ways in, easiest first. They all end the same way: a profile you can check.

## Way one: send one message

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, whatever you use:

`Prep me for a first call: I work at [your company], I'm meeting [person] at [account]. xray.joannatu.com/full`

If your AI can't open the link (Gemini, some days), use way two.

## Way two: paste the kit

Two pastes: the whole kit into a chat with web search on, then this line:

`MY COMPANY: [your company]. TARGET: [the account]. PERSON: [who you are meeting].`

## Way three: install it once

Five minutes in a Claude Project:

1. Paste `files/engine.md` into the project instructions, fill in your company.
2. Add `files/01` to `files/10` as project knowledge.
3. Type: `[account], meeting [person]`.

Custom GPT or Gemini Gem instead? The pack's guide covers both.

## Custom GPT or Gemini Gem

A custom GPT's instructions box caps at 8,000 characters and the engine is nearly twice that: add `files/engine.md` as the first knowledge file instead, with one instruction line: "Open engine.md first and follow it exactly." A Gemini Gem takes ten knowledge files and the kit is eleven: leave out `files/08-the-internal-case.md` and paste it in on the day you need it. Either way, the profile's "Files read" line should say "02 + full catalog"; that is how you know the install worked.

## Before you walk in

Somewhere in the profile, your AI got something wrong. Which AI you use decides how often. That is what the sources are for: check every line you plan to say out loud, before the room, not in it.

It can't read anything behind a login or a paywall.
