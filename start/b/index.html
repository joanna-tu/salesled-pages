Account X-ray: Aurelin a.s.

Files read: 01, 02 + full catalog, 03-10
Working assumption: [H] Cartesa sells a hosted commerce platform to retail and DTC brands; best fit where a brand's current stack limits speed or fragments across markets. Correct this line and I will rebuild on your version.
TARGET read as: Aurelin a.s., the Prague-listed jewellery retailer inside Karona Group. Not Aurelin Capital s.r.o., the Brno receivables-collection firm.
Meeting: Marek Holan, Ředitel e-commerce · Seller: Cartesa

Meeting card

The bet: Karona bought Amelot for 690 million crowns in January and now runs two jewellery brands on two entirely separate commerce stacks. Nobody has said publicly how that resolves, and the person who will have to live with the answer is sitting across the table.

Ask (menu, not script):

  1. Where does Amelot's online sit in your scope now?holding: Amelot closed in January and did about twenty-one million crowns online in its first quarter inside the group
  2. What would have to be true before Amelot and Aurelin share one customer view?holding: the two brands run separate storefronts and separate accounts today
  3. What makes the second brand harder to grow online than the first?holding: online was about a sixth of group revenue in March

Watch out: Do not call the current setup old, legacy, or a constraint. His team shipped the Czech app, the Austrian site and the Austrian app on it, the last one thirteen days ago.

Timing: ACT NOW. Freshest signal is the Austrian app launch, attached directly to his function. Next catalyst: the H1 report on 21 August, nine days out.

Real prep takes 40 minutes. You have 10.

  • You open the meeting invite 10 minutes before the call.
  • Their website says "AI-powered platform" and nothing else.
  • You guess at their priorities and open with a discovery question they have answered forty times.
  • You lose the first five minutes proving you did no homework.

Account X-ray compiles the facts. Then it hands you the angles.

The facts

A source on every line

The money, what just changed, who decides, the tool they already run. Behind it: 39 signals across filings, hiring, press and tech changes. Each line comes with its source and date, so you can check anything before you say it out loud.

The POVs

Five to seven angles to try

Points of view built from the facts it found, in a suggested order, with the risky ones flagged. Ideas to take into the meeting, not scripts.

The self-check

It grades its own work

Sixteen checks run before you see a word. Whatever it could not verify, it says so, and every hypothesis is labeled as one.

How deep it goes depends on which AI you run it in. Check the sources before the meeting; that is what they are for.

You could do this three other ways.

Doing it yourself across ten tabs

30 to 60 minutes per account. You do it for the big logos and wing the rest.

Pasting the company name into ChatGPT

Even with search on, it skims a page or two, dates nothing, and blends what it found with what it remembers. You cannot tell which line is real.

Sales intelligence platforms

Firmographics, intent scores and a seat licence. None of it tells you what to say in minute two.

Account X-ray

It makes your AI search the open web instead of answering from memory. Every line gets a source and a date, the POVs are built on what it found, and it admits what it could not check. It runs inside the AI subscription you already pay for.
