What the kit does

What comes back

Ten lines you can say out loud

The card you read in the lift, then the dossier: the money, what just changed, who is in the room, the tool you would be replacing. It closes with five to seven angles to open with.

What it reads

39 signals, three questions each

Filings, hiring, leadership moves, tech changes, expansion, reviews. Each signal gets the same three questions: is it my problem, is it fresh, who owns the decision. Then a verdict: act now, this week, or watch.

Why you can trust it

It grades its own work

Sixteen checks against stale or invented findings, run before you see a word, and it says so when it is guessing. It never reads LinkedIn. It sells nothing inside your prep. The whole method is public, on one page.
